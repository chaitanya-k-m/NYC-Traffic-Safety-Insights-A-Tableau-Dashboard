# NYC Traffic Safety Insights: A Tableau Dashboard  

## **Project Overview**  
This project presents a **comprehensive Tableau dashboard** that analyzes **New York City's traffic accident trends**, identifying high-risk areas, contributing factors, and patterns over time. By leveraging interactive data visualization techniques, the dashboard offers actionable insights for policymakers, urban planners, and public safety officials to enhance road safety measures.

## **Key Insights from the Dashboard**  
- **Accident Hotspots:** Identifies **high-risk intersections and boroughs** with recurring incidents.  
- **Time-Based Trends:** Analyzes accident frequency by **hour, day, and season** to highlight peak risk periods.  
- **Impact of External Factors:** Evaluates how **weather, lighting conditions, and road surface** affect accident severity.  
- **Vehicle & Pedestrian Safety:** Examines the impact of **vehicle type, speeding, and driver behavior** on injuries and fatalities.  

## **Dashboard Features**  
- **Interactive Heatmaps & Geospatial Analysis:** Pinpoints accident-prone areas with **geo-mapped data visualization**.  
- **Trend Analysis & Comparative Views:** Displays **year-over-year accident trends** and **comparisons across boroughs**.  
- **Filter & Drill-Down Capabilities:** Users can **customize views** based on time, weather, vehicle type, and borough.  

## **Technology & Tools Used**  
- **Tableau** – Designed an **interactive dashboard** for intuitive data exploration.  
- **Excel/CSV** – Cleaned and prepared data for seamless integration.  
- **Geospatial Mapping** – Used **heatmaps and location clustering** to identify accident concentration zones.  
- **Trend Forecasting** – Applied data visualization techniques to predict and analyze accident trends.  

## **Why This Matters**  
Traffic accidents remain a significant public safety concern in NYC, impacting **pedestrians, cyclists, and motorists**. By visualizing this data in an intuitive dashboard, stakeholders can **pinpoint dangerous intersections, assess risk factors, and make data-driven decisions** to improve urban road safety.
